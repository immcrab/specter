Questions families ask

Do sessions follow what's happening in class?

Yes. Students bring current assignments, quizzes, and notes. Tutors build the session around the material the teacher is actually grading.

Online or in person?

Both. Online sessions use a shared whiteboard and screen sharing; in-person sessions run at our study rooms on weekday afternoons and evenings.

How do parents stay informed?

Every session gets short notes, and families receive a monthly summary with goals, wins, and the next area of focus.

What if we need to reschedule?

Let us know 24 hours ahead and we'll move the session at no cost. Cancel any week without a registration fee.
